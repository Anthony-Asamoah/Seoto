Choose a method

i Simple interest. Interest is paid only on your original principal. You earn the same fixed amount every rollover — earnings never earn interest themselves.
i Compound interest. Each rollover your interest is added to the balance, so the next rollover earns interest on the principal plus all previously earned interest. Growth accelerates over time.
i Cumulative interest. Compound interest, but you also add a fresh principal deposit at the start of every rollover. Each new contribution joins the growing balance and compounds from then on — ideal for regular, recurring savings (a “susu”).
